Important points

Working days

  • Foundational stage: around 160–180 days.
  • Preparatory stage: around 180–190 days.
  • Middle and secondary stages: around 200–220 days.
  • These are academic-planner guidance ranges, not a confirmed count for this school.

Holidays

  • J&K Government holidays for April–December 2026 are marked in the calendar.
  • Weekly offs and school vacations are separate from the gazetted holiday list.
  • Moon-dependent festival dates may change.
  • 2027 holidays should be updated from the next official government order.

Examination schedule

  • Formative Assessment 1: first week of May 2026.
  • Formative Assessment 2: first week of August 2026.
  • Formative Assessment 3: third week of September 2026.
  • Formative Assessment 4: first week of November 2026.
  • Formative Assessment 5: second week of December 2026.
  • Formative Assessment 6: last week of January 2027.
  • Summative Assessment: second week of March 2027.
  • All are indicative windows from a suggestive planner, not confirmed school datesheets.

National festivals and observances

  • Independence Day: 15 August.
  • Teachers’ Day: 5 September.
  • Gandhi Jayanti: 2 October.
  • Children’s Day: 14 November.
  • Constitution Day: 26 November.
  • Republic Day: 26 January.

Religious and cultural festivals

  • Baisakhi, Navratri, Ram Navami and Dussehra.
  • Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Azha, Ashoora and Eid-i-Milad-ul-Nabi.
  • Janamashtami, Diwali, Guru Nanak Dev Ji’s Birthday and Christmas.
  • Check the calendar for the 2026 dates listed in the government order.

Other school activities

  • Science exhibitions and project competitions.
  • Sports meets and inter-house competitions.
  • Reading, art, heritage and environmental activities.
  • Parent–teacher meetings and student counselling.
  • Dates to be announced by the school.
