About Bhargav Public School

Established in 2001 under the guidance of Bhargav Trust, Bhargav Public School is located in Lower Gadi Garh, Satwari, Jammu, Jammu and Kashmir. The school is committed to making education accessible to every section of society.

Our Identity

Bhargav Public School provides education from Nursery to Class 10, with English as the medium of instruction. The school follows the Jammu and Kashmir Board of School Education (JKBOSE).

Our motto, “Education Is Liberation,” reflects our belief that education helps individuals develop knowledge, confidence, independent thinking and the ability to contribute positively to society.

Our History and Purpose

Bhargav Public School was established with a mission to educate every aspect of society. The school aims to make learning accessible to children from different backgrounds and to support their academic and personal development.

Since its establishment in 2001, the school’s purpose has been to provide an environment where students can learn, develop their abilities and prepare for the opportunities and responsibilities of life.

Our Commitment

We aim to support students through classroom learning, discipline, responsibility and the development of essential life skills. We believe that education should contribute not only to academic progress but also to the growth of thoughtful, capable and responsible individuals.

Bhargav Public School continues to work towards its founding purpose of making education a meaningful opportunity for every child.
