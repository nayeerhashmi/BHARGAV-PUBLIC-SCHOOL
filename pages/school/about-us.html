About our school

Established in 2001, Bhargav Public School is located in Lower Gadi Garh, Satwari, Jammu, Jammu and Kashmir. The school provides education from Nursery to Class 10.

Bhargav Public School is managed by a Management Committee under Bhargav Trust. Vikas Sharma serves as the Chairman of the Trust.

The school aims to provide a supportive learning environment that encourages academic growth, confidence, discipline, responsibility and the overall development of students. The school motto is “Education Is Liberation”.
