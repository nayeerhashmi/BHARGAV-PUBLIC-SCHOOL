Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Bhargav Public School handles information when you use this website, contact the school or submit feedback.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Bhargav Public School, located at Lower Gadi Garh, Satwari, Jammu, Jammu and Kashmir, aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy describes the website’s current forms and intended handling of information. It is not a guarantee that every technical or operational process is already configured, and it should be reviewed when the website or its service providers change.

1. Information you may provide

  • Admission enquiries: the form may ask for a parent or guardian’s name, student’s name, class sought, mobile number, optional email address and a message. The current form prepares an enquiry in your browser so you can copy it and contact the school. It does not automatically send the form to the school.
  • Feedback: the feedback form asks for your name, mobile number, feedback category and message. It is designed to verify a mobile number through a one-time password (OTP) and submit feedback to the school database when the required services are configured and working.
  • Messages sent directly: if you contact the school by phone, email or WhatsApp, the information you share may be processed by the relevant communication service and by the school to respond to you.
  • Technical information: the website host or service providers may process technical logs such as IP address, browser details, request time and pages requested as part of delivering and protecting the website. The school should confirm the exact logs and retention periods with its providers.

2. Why information may be used

Information may be used to respond to admission questions, review feedback, verify a mobile number, maintain website security, investigate misuse and meet applicable legal obligations. Information should not be used for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate basis and any notice or permission required by law.

3. Mobile verification and service providers

The feedback feature is designed to use MSG91 to send and verify OTP codes. If this feature is active, your mobile number and OTP request details are sent to that provider for verification. A submitted feedback entry may be stored in the school’s configured Cloudflare D1 database. Cloudflare also provides website hosting and related infrastructure. These providers process information under their own service terms and privacy documentation.

Never share an OTP with another person or include passwords, financial account details, identity document numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information in a feedback message.

4. Admission enquiry information

The current admission enquiry form prepares a message locally in your browser and lets you copy it for sending through a communication channel of your choice. Do not assume that an enquiry has reached the school until you send it and receive an appropriate confirmation. If you send the copied message by email or WhatsApp, that service’s privacy terms also apply.

5. Children and student information

This website is associated with a school serving children. Parents and lawful guardians should submit information about a child only when necessary for the enquiry and should use the school’s approved channels for official admission records. Please do not upload or send a child’s sensitive information through a general feedback message. The school should apply the parental consent, notice and other safeguards required by applicable law before processing children’s personal data.

6. Sharing of information

The school does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website or communication features, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the website and its users. Access to stored feedback should be limited to people who need it for an authorised school purpose.

7. Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or for a longer period where applicable law requires it. The school should set and periodically review specific retention periods for feedback records, technical logs and any future admission records. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure; appropriate access controls and safeguards should be maintained by the school and its providers.

8. Cookies, links and embedded services

The website may link to external services, including WhatsApp and school communication providers. When you open an external website or service, its own privacy policy and settings apply. Hosting providers may use technical mechanisms necessary to deliver, protect or troubleshoot the website. This policy should be updated if analytics, advertising cookies, embedded third-party content or additional tracking tools are introduced.

9. Your requests and questions

You may contact the school to ask about personal information you have submitted, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask for deletion where appropriate and legally possible. The school may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may need to retain certain records where required by law.
